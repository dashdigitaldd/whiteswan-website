04 — House Guide

The House, Explained

Tap any topic. If it's not here, message us — we answer fast.

Infinity Pool

Private and exclusively yours — never shared. Jets run automatically every hour (manual toggle in the pool pump room). Lights turn on at sunset, off at midnight. The pool is unheated but sits in full sun all day, keeping it comfortable year-round. You also have access to the community pool near the beach.

Beach Access

The private community beach is a 10-minute downhill walk or 1-minute drive: head down the road, take the first right, continue until you see stairs on the left, and follow them down. Beach chairs, tables, and umbrellas are in the plastic bench in the parking area. Cabanas and the community pool are yours to use.

Air Conditioning

AC in every bedroom plus two units downstairs — remotes are mounted near the light switches. Keep windows and doors closed while running. Not cooling? Check the remote's mode setting first, then message us and we'll send a technician.

WiFi

Network name and password are in your arrival message and posted inside the house. Issues? Reset the router using the instructions by the router, or message us.

Laundry Room

Next to the front door — the access code is in your arrival message. Washer, dryer, detergent, and extra towels are complimentary for stays of 2+ nights. Extra drinking water bottles are stocked here too.

TV & Streaming

Netflix and Disney+ are ready to go. For live sports, open Disney+ → ESPN section. Change Netflix language via the 'SPA' option in the bottom-right of the app. There's an outdoor TV too.

Drinking Water

Water dispenser under the stairs; extra bottles in the laundry room — grab them anytime. If supplies run out, leave the empties in the parking area and we'll arrange a refill.

BBQ, Bar & Outdoor Living

Gas BBQ with utensils next to the wet bar. If the gas runs out, message us — a replacement arrives promptly. Sun loungers, outdoor dining, alfresco shower, outdoor gym, and garden all yours.

Trash & Housekeeping

Place bags in the two black bins in the parking area — the community team collects them. Garden and pool staff may stop by occasionally; ask us if you'd prefer to skip a visit. Stays of 4+ nights include two complimentary cleaning shifts.

Safety & Small Fixes

First aid kit in the kitchen drawer. Plunger in the pool pump room. Anything broken or leaking? Send us a photo — we resolve it quickly and never make it awkward. Small outdoor lizards are harmless and keep bugs away.

07 — The Area

Explore Surf City

Our shortlist — tested by 200+ groups of guests.

10 min by car

El Tunco

World-famous surf town — shops, restaurants, nightlife, beach bonfires. Surf lessons run $30–50 for 1.5h with board included; mornings have the best waves.

Relaxed vibe, great restaurants, solid surf — and the birthplace of the Bitcoin circular economy.

Boardwalk with restaurants, shops, and carnival rides. The pier is the spot for fresh local seafood with ocean views.

A refreshing hike to waterfalls and natural pools — best with a local guide (see Tours above).

50 min by car

Stunning crater lake with lakeside dining — a scenic half-day trip. Full-day options: Tazumal, Joya de Cerén, and San Andrés archaeological sites.

08 — FAQ

Questions, Answered

The questions we get most.

When do I get the door code and exact address?

Three days before arrival, via Airbnb message. For security, codes are never published on this site.

Can we get a discount?

Our pricing already reflects the best available rate — private infinity pool, ocean views, direct beach access, and full-service hosting are all included. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Is the pool heated?

No — and it doesn't need to be. It gets full direct sun all day and stays comfortable year-round in this tropical climate.

Does Uber work there?

Yes, though availability can be limited. Tell the driver 'Residencial Xanadu, Casa F-2.' For reliability, our driver Gerardo is the better option — see Services.

Can we bring more than 10 people?

No — the community enforces a strict 10-guest maximum at the security gate, with no exceptions. Infants typically don't count toward the limit.

Can day visitors come by?

Only registered guests can enter the community — security checks everyone at the gate. If your plans change, message us and we'll see what's possible within the 10-guest limit.
