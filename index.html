05 — BEFORE YOU PACK

A few useful
little details.

Have something else in mind? We’re a message away.

Talk to us ↗
How do I book my stay?+

Start with Plan your stay to choose your preferred dates and group size on this website. While online booking is being connected, send your request to our host on WhatsApp. We confirm availability, the full price and terms before you commit; selecting dates does not reserve them.

Is the pool just for us?+

Yes. The villa and its infinity pool are private for your group.

How do we get to the beach?+

The community beach is reached by a downhill route with stairs. Ask us about the route before booking if anyone in your group has mobility needs.

Can you help plan something special?+

We can coordinate a chef, massages, groceries, transfers and celebration details. We confirm supplier availability and your quote before any service is booked.

Where are the arrival details?+

Our guest guide has general arrival information. Private access instructions are shared with confirmed guests through Airbnb before arrival.
