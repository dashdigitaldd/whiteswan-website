04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
before you arrive.

How do I book the villa?

View current accommodation rates and availability on our Airbnb listing. You can also send us an enquiry about your dates and plans. Direct payment through this website is not available yet.

Is the pool private?

Yes. The villa has its own private infinity pool for your group.

Can you arrange extras before we arrive?

Yes—ask about a chef, massages, groceries, transport or a celebration. We confirm supplier availability, the service details and your quote before any booking.

Where do I find arrival instructions?

The guest guide covers general arrival information. Door, Wi-Fi and safe access codes are sent only through Airbnb, three days before arrival. They are never published here.
