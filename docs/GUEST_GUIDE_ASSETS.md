# Original welcome-guide assets

The guest companion restores the photographs selected in Wendy and Sergio's [Canva welcome guide](https://whiteswan.my.canva.site/). These are the original image files referenced by the published Canva design, not replacement stock photographs or generated images.

## Provenance and processing

`content/guest-guide-assets.json` records each visible source page, Canva media ID, original image URL, source-file SHA-256, local asset path and dimensions. The 23 local images are in `assets/guest-guide-source/`. Source files were retrieved on 2026-10-23. Images were resized to fit within 1440 x 1440 pixels and compressed to WebP at quality 86. There are no retouches or generative edits. Total asset size is approximately 1.77 MB.

The page numbers follow the 18 visible pages. Canva also contains a hidden social-media page before the thank-you page; it is not counted.

## Original links and menus

The manifest retains original venue and destination links and published restaurant telephone numbers. No direct restaurant menu document is linked in the Canva guide itself. The chef service describes a six-course dinner and seafood, Mexican, Salvadoran and BBQ menu choices, but does not include a menu PDF.

Supplemental menu links are recorded separately from original-guide links:

- Pelícanos food and drinks menus are linked from [the restaurant's own website](https://www.pelicanosrestaurante.com/). Both open its hosted Flipsnack menu documents.
- Beach Break's [official menu page](https://www.thebeachbreakhotels.com/dining) is linked from its own navigation. Its published +503 6420 3740 number matches the Canva guide. The page also provides a PDF-menu button.

The repeated `jessiessupperclub.com` hyperlink attached to several restaurant addresses is an unrelated template link in the Canva design. It is excluded instead of being presented as a Salvadoran restaurant menu.

## Private material

Wi-Fi credentials and the Wi-Fi QR image are not part of this public asset collection. They must be supplied through the private guide response, never committed as a static public image or JSON property. Guest records, internal staff contacts and source operational documents are also excluded.

## Validation

All 23 images were opened as a contact sheet and matched to their original positioned Canva element. The image decoder verified every local file, dimensions and total size. No menu or venue link is represented as a service booking, order or message sent.
